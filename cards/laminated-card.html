⚑ AUTOMATIC NO-GO OVERRIDE CONDITIONS

If ANY condition below is met, the decision is NO-GO regardless of the total score:

  • Any single dimension's weighted score ≥ 90% of its maximum possible
  • Three or more dimensions with raw scores at or above their red-flag threshold
  • Item 2.1 (Study Visits) = 3 AND Item 2.3 (Procedures) = 3 simultaneously
  • Item 4.6 (Budget Adequacy) = 3 — budget clearly insufficient for safe conduct
  • Item 4.7 (Current Workload) = 3 — site at or over current capacity
  • Item 5.1 (Population Access) = 3 AND Item 5.2 (Enrollment Timeline) = 3
HOW TO COMPLETE AN ASSESSMENT
1
Score each item Rate every criterion 0–3 using the Front side descriptors.
2
Check red flags Any dimension raw score above its threshold triggers a warning.
3
Apply weights Each dim score is multiplied by its weight factor (1.0×–1.5×).
4
Normalize Sum all weighted scores ÷ 1.245 → final score on 0–100 scale.
5
Apply matrix Cross-reference final score band with Dim 5 raw band at right.
6
Check overrides Any override condition = automatic NO-GO regardless of score.
FINAL SCORE INTERPRETATION & THRESHOLDS
