Automatic NO-GO Override Criteria

  • Any single dimension weighted score ≥ 90% of its maximum
  • Three or more dimensions with raw score at red-flag threshold
  • Item 2.1 (Visits) = 3 AND Item 2.3 (Procedures) = 3 simultaneously
  • Item 4.6 (Budget Adequacy) = 3 (budget clearly insufficient)
  • Item 4.7 (Workload) = 3 (site at or over capacity)
